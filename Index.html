Our Heritage & Craft

Flavours Rooted in Kerala

Payyannur Restaurant brings the treasured coastal traditions and aromatic feasts of Kerala and South India straight to Abu Dhabi. Every dish celebrates authentic culinary heritage, from slow-roasted coconut beef preparations to delicate, hand-pressed breakfast breads.

By carefully sourcing native spices, fragrant curry leaves, pure coconut oil, and fresh ingredients, we honour the comfort of home-cooked Malabar meals while providing hospitable dine-in comforts and swift delivery across the Emirates capital.
